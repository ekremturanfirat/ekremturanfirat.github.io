PCB Motor with Internal Driver

October 2026 – Ongoing

METU Power Lab · Private repository

An axial-flux motor whose stator windings are traces on a 12-layer PCB, with the whole motor driver on a bay of the same board. The driver has three TI LMG2100 GaN half-bridges switching at 100 kHz (8.7 A rms, 15.4 A peak per phase), an STM32G491 MCU, linear Hall sensors plus an index Hall for the absolute rotor angle, CAN FD and an isolated USB-C debugger.

Power comes from a 12 V input with reverse-polarity and hot-swap protection, or from a USB-C PD charger. The rev A board (75 × 107.5 mm, 221 parts on both sides) is fully routed with a clean DRC.

GaN-Based High-Frequency Switched DC-DC Synchronous Buck Converter Unfinished

October 2025 – January 2026

AGH University of Krakow Power Electronics Lab · GitHub

An educational demo board for laboratory classes, where students can watch how the parts heat up as the switching frequency, load and voltage change. Under the supervision of Prof. Robert Stala, I worked out the electrical design (48 V input, 100 W output), selected the GaN power stage, microcontroller and temperature sensors, drew the schematic in Altium and started the PCB layout.

The project stopped at the design stage when my exchange ended. The files are shared as they are.
