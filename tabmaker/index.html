Options

Good to know

  • Click any spot in the tab to jump the song there. The highlighted column follows the music.
  • The fretboard under the player shows both hands. Left hand: the hand slides to its position and each finger presses its string (1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky; a long bar = barre); a ring at the nut = open string; dashed circles = the next notes. Right hand, by the sound hole: p thumb, i index, m middle, a ring on the strings being plucked.
  • Press Space to play / pause. Scroll yourself any time; “follow” waits a few seconds before it moves the page again.
  • Playing techniques: an arc joins a hammer-on or pull-off, a slanted line is a slide up / down, a curved arrow is a bend (½ = half step, full = whole step) and the arrow back down its release, a wavy line is vibrato. In the .txt they are written h, p, /, \, b, r and ~. They are guessed from the sound; set “Techniques” in Options to see more or none.
  • “Play the tab” plays back what the tab says, so you can compare it with the real song by ear. Tick “tab along” to hear the tab quietly on top of the song: wrong notes stand out.
  • “Compare with the song” plays the tab, listens to it the way it listened to the song, and matches the notes. Bar numbers turn green (sounds like the song), yellow or red (different); hover one to see which notes are missing or extra.
  • The AI hears notes, not hands: it picks the comfiest string/fret for each note, which may differ from the player’s.
  • Full-band songs: vocals, bass and keys get picked up too. Use “Melody only” or “Bass only” and the sensitivity slider to clean it up.
Notes are detected with Spotify's Basic Pitch model (Apache 2.0).
